# Review wrapper measurement before 0.6.0 (2026-11-20)

The tables are in [2026-11-20T143209Z.md](2026-11-20T143209Z.md), every run in the `.jsonl` beside
it. Measured on the 0.6.0 review (background runs, answer reuse, the shared access profile) on
macOS, Codex CLI 0.169.0 with `gpt-6.2-sol` at medium effort, Claude Code 2.1.328 with `opus[1m]`
(resolved to `claude-opus-5-5`); 60 runs, 5 per arm and range, arms in turning order.

| arm | recall | precision | wall s | prepare s | wait s | tokens | cost USD |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| codex-jst | 1.00 ± 0.00 | 0.99 ± 0.04 | 35 ± 5 | 0.2 | 0.0 | 116,474 ± 10,981 | not reported |
| codex-direct | 1.00 ± 0.00 | 0.95 ± 0.07 | 77 ± 49 | – | – | 325,556 ± 199,922 | not reported |

| claude-direct | 1.00 ± 0.00 | 0.99 ± 0.04 | 24 ± 7 | – | – | 163,891 ± 30,425 | 0.282 ± 0.027 |

What it shows:

- **Recall does not separate the arms here.** Every arm found every seeded defect in every run:
  the fixture is too easy for these models. A harder fixture (subtler defects, larger ranges, noise)
  is needed before anyone claims a difference in what is found.
- **Precision** is about the same; the direct arms report a few more findings outside the ground
  truth, most of them real (grep reading a pattern as an option, a failed export truncating the old
  report; see the list in the tables).
- **Speed and cost favour the wrapper.** Codex through `jst review` took 35 s against 77 s, with a
  tenth of the spread, and used about a third of the tokens; Claude Code took the same time and cost
  $0.086 against $0.282 (−70 %), with about a quarter of the tokens. The prepared patch, change
  index and view save the reviewers their own exploration.
- **jackstay's own stages are small**: building the view took 0.2 s and nothing waited for a slot
  or the start lock (one review at a time per arm).

Limits of this measurement: one machine, one fixture, five runs per cell; two other `jst review`
runs of this work ran at the same time for part of the hour (separate stores, so no slot was
shared, but the providers' load was); Codex's cost is not reported under a subscription, so only
its tokens are compared.
